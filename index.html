01THE PROBLEM

The ecological overdraft

An account with a living limit.

A bank balance can be drawn down. So can the natural systems that supply food, materials and a stable climate.

01 / INCOME

Biocapacity

Nature's income: the ecosystems' capacity to regenerate resources and absorb some wastes over time.

02 / SPENDING

Footprint

Humanity's spending: the demand we place on biologically productive land and sea.

03 / DEBT

Deficit

When spending exceeds renewal, the gap is an ecological debt paid through depleted stocks and stressed cycles.

How it's calculatedApproximate educational estimates

These simplified coefficients help show how changing an input can change an estimate. They are not a full life-cycle assessment; actual footprints depend on location, supply chains, technology, and accounting choices.

Carbon · kg CO₂e / day

Electricity: kWh × 0.71. Travel: km × selected mode factor (walk/cycle 0; bus 0.09; metro/train 0.04; two-wheeler 0.05; petrol car 0.17; diesel car 0.19; flight-heavy 0.25). Add diet/day (vegan 1.5; vegetarian 2.0; mixed 3.3; non-veg 4.5; heavy meat 7.0) and consumption/day (low 1.0; medium 2.5; high 5.0).

Water · liters / day

Direct water equals the slider value (blue). Diet/day (vegan 2,000; vegetarian 2,500; mixed 3,500; non-veg 4,500; heavy meat 6,000 L) is split 70% green, 15% blue and 15% grey. Shopping/day: low 300, medium 800, high 1,800 L (illustrative split). Electricity adds kWh × 2 L (blue).

Ecological · global hectares

Carbon part = (kg CO₂e/year ÷ 1,000) × 0.33. Food part: vegan 0.4, vegetarian 0.6, mixed 0.9, non-veg 1.3, heavy meat 1.9 gha. Built-up/consumption: low 0.1, medium 0.25, high 0.6 gha. Water part = (liters/day ÷ 10,000) × 0.05.

All inputs and results stay in this browser.Comparison values are approximate reference points.

06A NEW TOOL

The AI connection

A tool with a footprint, too.

Training and running AI models uses computing infrastructure, which draws electricity and requires equipment and cooling. The footprint depends on the model, hardware, grid and how the service is used—so there is no single universal cost per question.

AI can also help map forests from satellites, balance smart grids and improve climate modelling. Its value depends on where it is used, what energy powers it, and whether the benefits justify the material and energy costs.

COSTSCompute · electricity · water · hardware

Impacts vary by data centre, energy mix, cooling method and model scale.

USESForests · grids · climate models

Applications can support observation and planning, alongside human expertise.
